## Protected continuity

- Gloin Hammerfeld's ancestry / First Throne relationship remains genuinely undecided, even behind the scenes.
- The ongoing roleplay remains paused at Hadrik Ironvein's desk, Autumn Day 22, Fifth Age Era of Crowns, Year 3847, about 11:25 a.m.
- Orcs were created by the southeastern Avarran exiled dark mages during the Third Age; they did not exist in the First or Second Age.
- The Second Age has 1,437 years, with Awakening, Wonder, Fire being **Avarran**, not universal, era labels.
- The Third Age's 8,873 years and nine Avarran eras remain proposed as described in the current canon ledger.
- The sixfold world population model is 2,062,800,000 without double-counting the Seven Thrones inside the global Khazari total.
- The current 50,000-km planet diameter and 60% water / 40% land supersede older alternatives.
- Legacy dates, currency discrepancies and city-versus-domain population scope are explicitly unresolved where records disagree.
