# Eryndor — Post-Campaign-Reset Canon Audit
**Audit date:** 21 November 2026  
**Authority:** Explicit creator instruction: scratch everything Gloin did in the previous campaign; preserve established characters, locations, world history and lore, including the First Throne, the Silence Below and eastern mine.

## Result and scope
**Completed:** A repository-wide **source-inventory and active-canon reset audit**. Fetched and examined all **54/54** original archived Notion documents for titles, length and indicators of campaign material, plus all **149/149** curated encyclopedia entries in `data/world.json` for status and direct residual gameplay references. Corrected active encyclopedia entries that still carried retired Day 19–22 events as present-day facts. Preserved all original archive texts unchanged. The 54-document archive is not a complete original-chat export.

**Not claimed:** A line-by-line semantic reconciliation of every historical, geographical, economic and magical statement in approximately two million characters of archived text. Such a deeper historical audit remains open. This report is a completed **campaign-reset audit**, not a certificate that every worldbuilding contradiction has been resolved.

## Binding reset rules
1. **Retired:** Every previous action by Gloin and its consequences, including meetings, disclosures, investigations, lessons, apprenticeship, rune practice, purchases, coin ledger, quest progress, current location and precise Day 22 pause.
2. **Retained:** All established characters as independent inhabitants; their existing professions, identities, families and locations; world history, cultures, kingdoms, magic, geography, economy, institutions and other world lore.
3. **Retained but unproven:** First Throne history, Silence Below episodes and chronology, eastern mine and sealed structures, documentary discrepancies, missing engineers and political mysteries. No theory becomes fact because it appeared in the retired game.
4. **Never transfer automatically:** What Gloin learned, what he told NPCs, what NPCs did in response, his former funds, his appointments or any old event timestamps.
5. **No new campaign start established.** Gloin may remain an available character, but no replacement actions, inventory, schedule, apprenticeship or location have been authored.
6. **Ancestry unresolved:** Gloin's possible First Throne descent is neither affirmed nor denied and has no predetermined secret answer.

**Sources 31–37:** Living Atlases I–VII, including Gloin travel, political and social simulation. **Disposition:** Geography, societies, settlements, economies, factions and locations retained; any routes taken, appointments or personal outcomes for Gloin retired.

This is why archival preservation and current canon must be separate.

## Remaining creator decisions / contradictions
**R01 — Hammerfeld family names and relationships:** The curated encyclopedia currently names Durgan and Mara as Gloin's parents and Bruni as his older sister. Earlier campaign recollections instead named Durham as father and Bruni as mother. Because the user explicitly kept established characters, **neither version has been erased from the historical record**. The curated roster is the present working roster; this conflict requires an explicit creator ruling or verified later user correction.
**R02 — First Throne versus Seven Thrones:** The ancient First Throne and present seven independent Ironspine kingdoms both exist in lore. Their exact dynastic succession and founding dates are not established. Do not turn Second Age mountain guilds into founding charters of the Seven Thrones without a decision.
**R03 — Silence Below date anchors:** ~3,240 / 3,190 / 3,160 have incompatible “years ago” versus “before the Seven Thrones” reference points. Keep the sequence and uncertainty; do not assign precise dates.
**R04 — Eastern mine timing:** The old 63-years-ago closure reference is tied to the former campaign's calendar. Preserve the historical closure and relative-age note provisionally; establish a world-present anchor before treating it as a current elapsed interval.
**R05 — Historical authoritativeness:** The original Bible and Edition II contain both superseded wording and newer creator corrections. The source vault is evidence, not a second competing canon.
**R06 — Second Age local era names:** 1–321 Awakening, 322–1321 Wonder, 1322–1437 Fire are Avarran period names. Other continents retain independent nomenclature.
**R07 — Orc origins:** **Superseded by creator correction 21 Nov 2026:** Orcs/Urakai were created **sometime in the Second Age**, not the Third; the precise date is undecided. The southern dark mages and their ambitions remain associated with Orc creation. Their documented emergence in the Era of Fire (Years 1322–1437) creates a dating dependency to resolve, not permission to invent a date. No First Age Orcs.
**R08 — First Age:** One continuous Age, duration unknown, with no surviving reliable written records. **Multiple moons colliding is now the canonical initiating cause of the Great Cataclysm** (creator correction 21 Nov 2026), although exact moon count, sequence and downstream mechanisms remain open.
**R09 — Planet and census:** Current 50,000 km diameter, 60% water / 40% land and sixfold planning populations supersede older smaller planet and lower census. Retain proposed versus canon qualifications.
**R10 — Unresolved cosmology:** Lunar magic sources, the World's Breath and the divine framework must be reconciled using explicit creator decisions, not assumed to be one identical force.
**R11 — Full dialogue completeness:** Original Gloin chat transcripts and other-assistant continuations are not wholly represented by 54 archived Notion files. This no longer blocks worldbuilding, but missing dialogue cannot be invented.
**R12 — Independent NPC prehistory:** Former gameplay interactions do not become their backstories. Any relationships that predate gameplay must be supported independently or left undecided.

## Working editorial policy
**Canon** = explicit creator-approved world fact. **Working** = detailed, revisable lore. **Open** = deliberately undecided. **Disputed** = conflicting accounts/chronology. **Reported** = attributed allegation. **Non-canon** = superseded or retired gameplay. Every subsequent source update should honor these distinctions. Old archives remain readable, but the curated World Bible is the active reference.
